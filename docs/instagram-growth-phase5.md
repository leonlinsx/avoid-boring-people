# Bounded creative learning

Phase 5 extends the existing graphics-only factory and ABP-CREATIVE-001 trials.
It retains automatic source-backed admission, the rolling publication ceiling,
21 weekly render reservations, account identity, signatures, duplicate controls,
GPU fault guard, free-storage limits and existing schedules. Nothing changes
newsletter sends or @leon.lin.s.

## Payoff → explanation → decision question

The `compression` factor compares the existing attributed source-first opening
(which repeats its first sentence in the body) with a version that speaks every
original source sentence exactly once, in order. It retains the complete
paragraph, conditions and spoken author attribution. A fixed practical question
and a shorter follow CTA replace generic paragraph framing. Original cards show
the choice, its topic and a question about reconsidering it. These are prompts,
not extra factual assertions or a claim that the author's opinion is proven.

The narrow additional source lane accepts a complete one-sentence paragraph,
never an excerpt cut from a longer paragraph. Existing word, rights, publication,
entity, numeric, sensitive-domain and missing-context exclusions still apply.
Single claims additionally reject deictic references and unexplained stages,
theories or findings. Only the compact treatment can compile this lane. It
retains its three-distinct-claims arm ceiling; source shortage never expands it.

The consumer reconstructs the script from the owned article independently of the
producer. Changed claims, prompts, graphics or metadata fail reconstruction.
Independent ASR must preserve qualifications. Existing signed inventory uses
only the exact previous protected-main policy/runtime pair; that compatibility
cannot authorize the new compression structure. Unknown or mixed pairs fail.

## Weekly selection

The existing weekly scorecard stores `creative_learning` on the existing state
branch. The factory reads it automatically; no approval interface, new service,
model call or recurring chat is needed. Fresh reviews can change tie ordering
inside exploration; controls, the 60/25/15 policy, arm caps, source/topic blocking
and the positive-follower/multi-article exploitation gate are preserved.

Diagnostics use timely 24-hour observations from the preceding fourteen days.
Six posts across three articles are required to trigger either diagnosis:

- All reach values below 100: prioritize audience positioning and hooks, and
  flag Instagram-native discoverability for investigation after distinct
  treatments receive exposure.
- Six sufficiently exposed posts with available skip rates averaging at least
  65%: prioritize the nonrepeating explanation test.

These are exploratory screening rules, not significance tests. Sparse, missing,
late or stale evidence keeps balanced trials. An eight-day-old review cannot
control production. The report records evidence concepts, hypotheses, the
reason for selection and uncertainty. Equal-age 48-hour and seven-day outcomes
continue separately. Account followers remain the primary KPI and cannot be
allocated to overlapping Reels. The API does not establish follower authenticity
or supply attributable site/signup outcomes; these remain unavailable.

## Operating limits

Run the existing factory and hosted weekly commands; no new activation switch is
required beyond #159's enabled-by-default creative trials. Already admitted
assets keep their immutable scripts. New claims enter future production when
render budget and source inventory permit. Do not reset the weekly budget,
rerender published claims, or count disposable verification clips as live queue
inventory. The fourteen-day target is up to 42 publications, never a quota.
An empty source pool, failed admission, GPU hold or unavailable free storage
halts replenishment visibly.
